## Description
Warfare Fitness hands you a real training program and tells you exactly what to do today.

Answer two minutes of questions and you get a program matched to your goal, experience and equipment. Each session is written out: the exercises, the sets, and the weight to beat from last time. Log a meal from a photo. Track fasts, habits, streaks and levels. Connect Apple Health and see your steps, sleep and heart rate next to your training.

• 14 structured programs: fat loss, muscle, strength, calisthenics and military selection prep
• Every set logged: weight, reps, rest timer and a demo video for each exercise
• Food analysis from a photo, and barcode scanning
• Calories, protein and water targets set for you
• Steps, sleep and heart rate from Apple Health
• Fasting timer with stages
• Habit tracking, streaks, levels and quests
• A weekly coach report on your training and nutrition
• Dark mode and light mode

Warfare Fitness is not a medical service. Check with a healthcare professional before starting a new training or nutrition plan.

## App Privacy (data collection answers)
- Contact info: email address, name (account) — linked to the user, used for app functionality.
- Health & fitness: health data from Apple Health with consent, fitness data (workouts logged) — linked to the user, app functionality.
- User content: photos (meal analysis), messages in Community — linked to the user, app functionality.
- Identifiers: user ID — app functionality.
- Usage data / analytics: only if the cookie banner is accepted on the web; declare "Product interaction" for analytics.
- Data is not sold. No third-party advertising inside the app.
